ABOUT THE SCHOOL

70 Years of Excellence

Established in 1956, B.J. Mnyanda has grown as an important educational institution in KwaZakhele, Gqeberha.

Our History

B.J. Mnyanda Public Primary School is a no-fee public school situated on Njoli Road in KwaZakhele, Gqeberha (formerly Port Elizabeth), South Africa. The school operates under the Eastern Cape Department of Education and is a Quintile 3 institution.

Established in 1956, the school is celebrating its 70 Years of Excellence milestone in 2026. It has served generations of learners and remains a trusted educational pillar in the local community.

Our Community

The school participates in community and educational initiatives, including learner safety campaigns and local municipal engagement. Learners have participated in the National Schools Anti-Bullying Pledge, promoting safe, respectful and drug-free learning environments.
