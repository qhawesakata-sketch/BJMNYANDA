70th Anniversary

B.J. Mnyanda Public Primary School was established in 1956 and is celebrating 70 Years of Excellence in 2026.

The school is located on Njoli Road in KwaZakhele, Gqeberha, and serves the surrounding community as a Quintile 3 no-fee public primary school.
